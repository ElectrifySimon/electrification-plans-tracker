National Electrification Plans

National plans, roadmaps and strategies published in the run-up to COP31. 10 countries with a published 2026 plan, plus one regional bloc and 6 watchlist entries. As of 20 Nov 2026.

Whole-economy plan 6

France Europe · 23 Apr 2026 · Primary

Plan d'electrification des usages (Electrifions la France)

Government of France, Ministry of Economy and Finance

22 measures across nine ministries to move transport, buildings and industry off gas and oil. A second phase of 17 measures followed on 15 October 2026.

Source

Canada North America · 14 May 2026 · Primary

Powering Canada Strong: A National Strategy for an Electrified Canadian Economy

Natural Resources Canada

National electrification strategy plus an action plan. CAD 115 billion for electrification to 2041-42, including CAD 52 billion in Budget 2026. Aims to double electricity supply by 2050.

Source

China Asia · 01 Jun 2026 · Secondary

15th New-type Energy System Five-Year Plan

Government of China

Sets an indicative target of 35% electricity in final energy consumption by 2030, up from 30% in 2025. Repeated in the New-type Power System Five-Year Plan in August 2026. Document date is month-level.

Source

Turkiye Middle East · 05 Nov 2026 · Secondary

Milli Enerji 2040

Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources

Long-term national energy roadmap launched five days before COP31, built around electrification. Reported by trade press, not yet available as a published PDF.

Source

United Kingdom Europe · 12 Nov 2026 · Primary

The UK's clean electrification roadmap

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

Brings networks, buildings, transport and industry into one national account of the shift to clean electricity, framed against the COP31 35-by-35 objective.

Source

Ireland Europe · 16 Nov 2026 · Primary

Ireland's Electrification Roadmap, Phase 1

Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment

Maps about 130 existing policies, programmes and regulatory measures across heat, transport and industry. Phase 1 is a baseline; a Phase 2 action plan is to follow.

Source

Sectoral plan 1

Nepal Asia · 15 Jul 2026 · Secondary

National Transport Policy 2026 (2083 BS) and National Strategy for Electrification of Public Transport

Ministry of Physical Infrastructure and Transport

Declares an EV Decade, prioritises electric mass transit and sets the EV policy framework. Transport sector only, not a whole-economy plan. Date is month-level.

Energy access plan 3

Zambia Africa · 15 Mar 2026 · Primary

Rural Electrification Master Plan 2.0 (2025-2030)

Ministry of Energy

Successor to the 2008 master plan, setting rural electrification sequencing and targets to 2030. Date is month-level.

Source · PDF

Ethiopia Africa · 27 Oct 2026 · Secondary

National Electrification Program 3.0 (NEP 3.0)

Ministry of Water and Energy

Universal access by 2035, about 4.4 million off-grid connections, and a financing need above USD 18 billion, roughly 61% expected from external partners.

Source

Mozambique Africa · 06 Nov 2026 · Secondary

National Geospatial Least-Cost Electrification Plan 2026-2030

Ministry of Mineral Resources and Energy

Assigns each settlement to grid, mini-grid or standalone solar as the cheapest route to power, launched with an integrated electrification platform.

Source

Regional bloc 1

European Union Europe · 17 Jul 2026 · Primary

Electrification Action Plan, COM(2026) 595

European Commission

Indicative target of 46% electricity in final energy by 2040, against about 23% today, with 32% by 2030 as reference. A bloc instrument, not a country plan.

Source · PDF

Watchlist 6

Korea, Republic of Asia · 11 Nov 2026 · Secondary

No national electrification plan identified

Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment

Endorsed the 35-by-35 pledge on 11 November 2026. Published a 10-year (2026-2035) carbon neutrality and growth plan worth KRW 1,000 trillion, but it is an energy transition plan, not an electrification plan.

Source

Indonesia Asia · 30 Oct 2026 · Secondary

RUEN 2026-2035 (draft, public consultation)

Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources

The national energy plan revision was still in public consultation on 30 October 2026, with adoption by presidential regulation pending. Not yet published.

Source

Philippines Asia · 01 Nov 2026 · Secondary

Total electrification roadmap (in preparation)

Department of Energy with BARMM

DOE and the Bangsamoro government are developing a consolidated total electrification roadmap against a 100% electrification goal. No published national plan found.

Source

Peru Latin America · 01 Apr 2026 · Primary

Plan Nacional de Electrificacion Rural (PNER)

Ministry of Energy and Mines, DGER

Long-running national rural electrification plan, targeting about 90% rural coverage in 2026. Not a new 2026 publication.

Source

Rwanda Africa · 01 Jun 2026 · Primary

Revised National Electrification Plan (under approval)

Ministry of Infrastructure and Rwanda Energy Group

The revised NEP was expected to be approved in 2026 under the Mission 300 compact, targeting a 75% grid and 25% off-grid mix.

Source · PDF

Kenya Africa · 14 Jul 2026 · Primary

Kenya National Electrification Strategy (KNES 2025) with compact implementation roadmap

Ministry of Energy and Petroleum

KNES 2025 is in force and being operationalised through the Mission 300 compact, targeting 5.1 million additional household connections and 100% access by 2030.

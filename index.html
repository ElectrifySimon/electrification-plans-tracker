Scope: documents that set out a national electrification plan, roadmap or strategy. Whole-economy plans cover end-use electrification across transport, buildings and industry. Energy access plans cover first-time connections. Regional bloc instruments are labelled and not counted as countries.

Rows marked watchlist are entries that are in preparation, existing but not new in 2026, or where no national electrification plan was found. Entries built from trade or press reporting rather than the publisher's own document are marked secondary in the detail panel.
